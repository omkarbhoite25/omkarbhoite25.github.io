COSMIC PHENOMENA

The universe is filled with extraordinary events that push the boundaries of physics.

Black Holes

Regions of spacetime where gravity is so strong that nothing, not even light, can escape. The event horizon marks the point of no return, while the singularity at the center challenges our understanding of physics.

Explore more

Neutron Stars

The collapsed cores of massive stars, incredibly dense remnants spinning at extraordinary speeds. A teaspoon of neutron star material would weigh billions of tons on Earth.

Explore more

Supernovae

The explosive deaths of massive stars, briefly outshining entire galaxies. These cosmic explosions forge heavy elements and seed the universe with the building blocks of planets and life.

Explore more

Quasars

Extremely luminous active galactic nuclei powered by supermassive black holes. These cosmic beacons can be seen across billions of light years, revealing the universe's distant past.
