Cosmic Evolution

From the Planck epoch to the heat death of the universe, we trace the narrative of existence itself.

The Primordial Singularity

t = 0

Infinite density. Infinite temperature. The universe begins with a quantum fluctuation in the false vacuum.

Cosmic Inflation

t = 10^-36 s

Space expands exponentially by a factor of 10^26 in 10^-33 seconds, smoothing out the universe.

Epoch of Recombination

t = 380,000 years

Electrons combine with nuclei to form neutral atoms. The universe becomes transparent to radiation. The CMB is released.

Cosmic Dawn

t = 200 million years

The first stars ignite, forging hydrogen and helium into heavier elements. Their ultraviolet light begins reionizing the intergalactic medium.

Galaxy Assembly

t = 1 – 3 billion years

Galaxies merge and grow through hierarchical assembly. The cosmic web of dark matter filaments guides matter into vast clusters and superclusters.

Birth of the Solar System

t = 9.2 billion years

A molecular cloud collapses to form our Sun and protoplanetary disk. Within 100 million years, the rocky and gas giant planets take shape.

The Present Day

t = 13.8 billion years

Humanity peers back across 13.8 billion years of cosmic history. Dark energy accelerates the expansion as the universe races toward an uncertain fate.
